Tap empty area to spawn. Tap object to select. Swipe to rotate scene. Pinch to scale selected object.

How to use

  1. Allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Tap empty space to place a decoration.
  3. Tap a decoration, then pinch to scale it.
  4. Swipe left/right to rotate the whole scene.
  5. Use Screenshot or Record for sharing.

Best results: use mobile Safari/Chrome and good lighting.
